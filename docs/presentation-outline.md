# Smile Bengaluru clinic app: walkthrough presentation

**Audience:** the clinic's doctors, reception and assistants.
**Length:** about 20 minutes, plus questions.
**Goal:** every person leaves knowing where their daily work lives in the app, and tries it themselves in the first week.

Each slide below has three parts. **On the slide** is the text to put on screen, kept short. **Say** is the talking point. **Show live** is what to open in the app at that moment, where there is one.

---

## Slide 1: Title

**On the slide**

- Smile Bengaluru clinic app
- One page for every patient
- Walkthrough for doctors, reception and assistants

**Say**

Today we go through one patient's visit from start to finish, and where your part of it lives in the app.

---

## Slide 2: What changes for you

**On the slide**

- Before: patient details in one place, bills in another, notes on paper
- Now: one page per patient with everything on it
- Anything you type can be edited, and nothing is lost

**Say**

You told us the old system felt simple. The aim is the same: fewer places to look, and nobody ever stuck because they made a typo.

---

## Slide 3: One patient, one page

**On the slide**

- Search by name, phone, patient ID or file number
- Tabs: Overview, Vitals and Charts, Medical History, Appointments, Prescriptions, Bills, X-Rays, Treatment Records, Perio Chart, Documents, Consultations
- Allergies show in red at the top, every time

**Say**

Everything you need is a tab on the same page. Allergies are at the top so they are seen before anything is done.

**Show live**

Open a patient. Click through two or three tabs. Point at the red allergy strip.

---

## Slide 4: Edit anything, lose nothing

**On the slide**

- Every screen can be corrected
- The previous version is kept in the background
- Mistakes: Edit, Undo, Trash, Merge duplicates

**Say**

If a bill is wrong, fix the bill. If a consent form has a wrong name, correct it. The old version is kept, so a dispute can still be settled later.

**Show live**

Edit a note on a test patient and save.

---

## Slide 5: Start of day

**On the slide**

- Dashboard: new enquiries, today's appointments, recalls due, follow-up calls
- Bookings: every enquiry from the website and WhatsApp
- Call every row marked NEEDS STAFF first

**Say**

Reception opens these two screens first thing. The Dashboard says what is happening today. Bookings says who is waiting to hear from us.

**Show live**

Open the Dashboard, then Bookings.

---

## Slide 6: Registering a patient

**On the slide**

- Only a name is needed
- Type age as of today. The app keeps it up to date.
- Add the clinic file number if there is a paper file
- A warning appears if the person may already exist

**Say**

Do not delay a patient for missing details. Register the name now and add the rest later. If the app warns about a possible duplicate, check before saving.

**Show live**

Patients, then New patient. Type just a name and save.

---

## Slide 7: Appointments

**On the slide**

- New appointment: patient, doctor, treatment, time
- Edit or reschedule at any time
- Mark completed: the next check-up reminder is set for you
- Print the day sheet for the doctors

**Say**

Marking a visit completed is the one click that matters. It closes the visit and schedules the next recall automatically.

**Show live**

Book an appointment, reschedule it, mark it completed.

---

## Slide 8: In the chair: vitals

**On the slide**

- Assistants record BP, pulse, temperature, SpO2, blood sugar, weight
- A worrying reading puts a warning strip on the patient's page
- Doctors see it before starting

**Say**

Assistants take the readings, because you are the ones holding the cuff. If a number is worrying, the warning appears at the top of the patient's page, next to allergies.

**Show live**

Record BP 170 over 105 on a test patient. Go back to the Overview and show the warning.

---

## Slide 9: History and consultation

**On the slide**

- Medical history: conditions, allergies, medicines, pregnancy
- Consultation: complaint, findings, diagnosis, treatment, next visit
- Typo: Edit. Added later: dated amendment.

**Say**

For doctors. Each consultation saves the medical history as it stood that day. If a note needs a correction, edit it. If something is added after the visit, use a dated amendment so the record shows when.

**Show live**

Open Consultations. Show Edit and Add dated amendment.

---

## Slide 10: Plan, estimate, consent

**On the slide**

- Treatment plan: stages with costs, patient signs to approve
- One row of buttons: Create invoice, Share, Print estimate, Sign consent
- Under 18: a guardian signs, and the app enforces it

**Say**

A plan is a list of stages. From the plan you can raise the bill, send the estimate on WhatsApp, print it and take consent without leaving the screen.

**Show live**

Open a plan. Click Create invoice, then Sign consent.

---

## Slide 11: Bills and receipts

**On the slide**

- New invoice, or Create invoice from the plan
- Record payment: enter the total received so far
- Every payment gets its own numbered receipt, with the amount in words
- Share the bill or receipt on WhatsApp

**Say**

Reception. The one rule: when you record a payment, enter the total received so far, not only today's amount. Two thousand earlier plus three thousand today means enter five thousand.

**Show live**

On the test patient: New invoice for 5,000. Record 2,000, print the receipt. Record again with 5,000, print the second receipt.

---

## Slide 12: Printouts

**On the slide**

- Case sheet: one page for the paper file
- Summary: everything done, planned and billed
- Label: patient ID and file number for the folder
- Receipt, prescription, consent form, day sheet

**Say**

When a patient asks for their whole record, or is referred to someone else, print the Summary.

**Show live**

Open Case sheet and Summary for the same patient.

---

## Slide 13: The WhatsApp assistant

**On the slide**

- Answers the clinic number day and night
- Gives the clinic hours and open slots
- Books, cancels and reschedules for known patients
- New people leave an enquiry for the front desk

**Say**

The assistant works from the patient's phone number. If the number is saved on their record, it can book and cancel for them. An unknown number can only leave an enquiry.

**Show live**

From a number saved on a test patient, send: Can I book tomorrow at 11? Then show the appointment on the Appointments screen.

---

## Slide 14: Safety: symptoms go to a person

**On the slide**

- No medical advice from the assistant
- Pain, bleeding, swelling, fever, broken tooth: staff are alerted
- A NEEDS STAFF row appears on Bookings
- Someone must ring the patient

**Say**

The assistant records the message and tells the patient the team will contact them. It cannot treat anyone. Every NEEDS STAFF row needs a phone call from a person today.

**Show live**

Send: my gums are bleeding badly. Show the alert on the staff phone and the new row on Bookings.

---

## Slide 15: Who can do what

**On the slide**

- Doctors: clinical records, prescriptions, plans, consent, merging duplicates
- Reception: patients, appointments, bills, payments, receipts
- Assistants: the same as reception, plus vitals
- Admin: everything, plus staff and settings

**Say**

A grey button is not broken. It means your role does not do that. Clinical records are limited to doctors and admin on purpose.

---

## Slide 16: Not switched on yet

**On the slide**

- Reminders and recall messages sent by the clinic: waiting for Meta to approve two message templates
- Receipts and plans are sent as text on WhatsApp, not as PDF files
- Everyone should have their own login

**Say**

Be clear about what is coming. Reminders go live once the templates are approved. Until then the assistant only answers people who message first. Logins: right now everything is entered under admin accounts, so we cannot tell who recorded what. Each doctor should have their own.

---

## Slide 17: Your first week

**On the slide**

- Reception: book every appointment in the app
- Reception: raise every bill and receipt in the app
- Assistants: record vitals for every patient
- Doctors: log every treatment, and try the plan screen once
- Everyone: tell us what is slow or confusing

**Say**

The aim for the first week is that the daily loop lives here. If something is slower than the old way, tell us which step and we will fix it.

---

## Slide 18: Questions

**On the slide**

- The user guide is the document to keep open at the desk
- Report a problem with a screenshot and what you were trying to do

---

## Appendix A: Demo script

Use a test patient called Test Patient, with your own WhatsApp number saved on their record.

1. Open Test Patient. Show the tabs.
2. Record vitals: BP 170 over 105. Show the warning strip on the Overview.
3. Create a plan with two stages. Show Create invoice and Sign consent.
4. Invoice for 5,000. Record 2,000 and print the receipt. Record 5,000 total and print the second receipt.
5. Open Case sheet and Summary.
6. From the test number, send: Can I book tomorrow at 11?
7. Send: Cancel my appointment.
8. Send: my gums are bleeding badly. Show the staff alert and the NEEDS STAFF row on Bookings.
9. Open the WhatsApp screen under Admin and show the conversation.

## Appendix B: Before the demo

- Run the whole script once an hour before.
- Delete test rows from Bookings.
- Do not scroll the full patient list on a shared screen.
- Do not open Patient Import, Data Export or Trash.
- Do not click Send on Recall Reminders or Campaigns.
- Keep screenshots of a good run as a fallback.
